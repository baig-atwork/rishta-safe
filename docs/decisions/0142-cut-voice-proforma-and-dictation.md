# ADR 0142: Cut: voice-to-proforma and dictation

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-20
- **Deciders:** lane C (journey and explain), for the controller

## Context
Research 05: Gulf Arabic word error rates of 27 to 60 percent, 53 percent on Urdu-English code-switching, and no real consultation audio may be used, so no accuracy could be measured honestly.

## Decision
The clinician proforma is a typed, enumerated form with one optional free-text note (2,000 characters). Nothing in the journey lane reads audio or a transcript.

## Consequences
No speech path exists to attack or to mis-transcribe a negation.

## Revisit trigger
A spike on consented, native-dialect audio with key-term recall of at least 90 percent and no invented sentence on silence.
