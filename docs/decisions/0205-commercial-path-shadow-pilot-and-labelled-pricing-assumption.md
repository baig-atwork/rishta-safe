# ADR 0205: Commercial path: shadow pilot first, pricing only as a labelled assumption

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-20
- **Deciders:** the declarations lane (G1), under the architecture contract (rules 1, 2 and 14)

## Context
The judging criteria include commercial viability and a path to pilot, and the Context note asks for a buyer map and a pilot protocol with KPIs taken from the challenge's own measures. A pilot needs a licensed facility (Decree-Law Art. 28), ethics determination by the DoH research ethics committee (R04 section 2.5) and the clinical lead's sign-off. No buyer was contacted. A web search on 2026-11-20 found no published price for premarital screening explanation software in the UAE or the Gulf. The one directly readable comparable is a vendor price page for a genetic counselling and pharmacogenomics service platform. The mandatory service costs the couple only AED 100 to 250 in a 2024 press report.

## Decision
Propose a shadow pilot with a licensed facility, with ethics and sign-off as gates and the measures taken from the programme's own 90 percent referral KPI, time to counsellor brief, teach-back comprehension and a privacy audit. Never count cancelled marriages or any single choice as success. State pricing only as a labelled assumption with its basis and what could not be verified, and state no revenue figure. Name payers as hypotheses.

## Consequences
The commercial document is short and testable, and it invites correction by a real partner's quote. It will not impress a reader looking for market-size numbers, which we have no honest way to produce.

## Revisit trigger
A pilot partner or buyer gives a quote, a tender or a view on willingness to pay.
