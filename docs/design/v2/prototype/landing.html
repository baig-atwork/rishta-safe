Premarital screening companion · UAE · prototype on synthetic data

Read a screening result when you choose, in both your languages.

Rishta Safe explains a reported premarital genetic screening result in plain words, side by side in each partner's language. Each person decides who sees what. Anything high-impact goes to a qualified person.

Open the case benchSee the evidence →

The threshold

Nothing opens until each person chooses how to meet it.

Other tools show the result on arrival. Rishta Safe asks first, once, and records a refusal as a legal right, not as a dead end.

Read it nowThe result opens here. You can close it at any time.
Read it with a counsellor firstIt stays closed here until after your counselling visit.
Not yetNothing is recorded. We ask again next time.
I prefer not to knowRecorded as your right not to know. You can withdraw it.

Right not to know: Federal Decree-Law 49/2023, Art. 9(2). PUT /v1/couples/{id}/right-not-to-know. The screening centre and the national genomic database still hold the result (Art. 26(2)); this choice is about what you read here.

Who sees what

Each person decides what the partner sees. The law decides the rest.

Every cell is computed from the consent ledger and the emirate's disclosure rule, and every change you make writes a row to a hash-chained audit log.
