# Data declaration

Rishta Safe is a prototype running on made-up data. The organisers supply no data, their FAQ asks teams to "declare every data source", and their Terms bar content protected by third-party rights. This page lists every source used and every source deliberately not used. A source that is not listed here is not used.

## In one paragraph

No real patient, couple, hospital, laboratory or programme data was used, collected or seen at any point. Every person, name, couple, test result and laboratory value in the demo is synthetic. No dataset is downloaded or bundled: facts are cited and tables are hand-written.

## What is used

- **Hand-written tables.** The panel, condition and haematology rule tables are small and written by hand for this prototype. Every row carries a citation (title, year, URL or DOI) with the table or wording it came from, or is flagged illustrative and shown as illustrative on screen. Every clinical rule, cut-off and detection rate carries `needs_clinician_signoff: true`, and every Arabic or Urdu string carries `language_status: draft`.
- **Published figures as cited facts only.** Where a prevalence or carrier figure appears, it appears as a number with its citation, never as a copied table. An example of the kind of source is the Dubai premarital screening study of UAE nationals (Hemoglobin 2013, DOI 10.3109/03630269.2013.791627). No UAE programme statistic is generated by us (Federal Decree-Law 49/2023, Art. 25(3)).
- **Public guideline thresholds, cited with section references and not copied.** The NHS England sickle cell and thalassaemia screening handbook for antenatal laboratories, fifth edition, updated 10 October 2024, carries the Open Government Licence v3.0 (checked on its page footer, 2026-11-20). The Italian SITE best-practice paper on first and second level haemoglobinopathy diagnosis (J Clin Med 2022;11(18):5426, DOI 10.3390/jcm11185426) is licensed CC BY. Thresholds from the British Society for Haematology 2023 guideline, the Thalassaemia International Federation short guide and the Oman 2018 premarital guideline are cited as facts only; their licences were not checked, so none of their text is reproduced.
- **Our own mathematics.** Mendelian couple states, and a correction for related couples whose test misses are shared by descent. The derivation is ours and has not been reviewed by a statistician (Rishta Safe authoring note, pending clinical sign-off).
- **Synthetic couples.** Generated by gene dropping through defined pedigrees (unrelated, first cousins and others) with a fixed seed, so the true state of every synthetic couple is known. The carrier frequencies and detection rates used are assumed or illustrative and are labelled that way.
- **The two emirate standards, as structural references.** Pathway states, times and disclosure rules were read from the DoH Abu Dhabi Premarital Screening Standard V3 (DoH/STD/PSS/V3, September 2024) and the DHA Standards for Premarital Screening Services V1 (DHA/PHPD/ST-01, effective 27 March 2026). Their forms, report layouts, risk statements and gene list are not copied: the DHA text says its contents "shall not be copied or reproduced in any form without prior written permission", and the DoH document states no reuse licence. Our wording is original and cites the clause it follows.
- **Original closure instruments.** The eight patient items and the 12-field clinician proforma are written by us. The UK PRSB outpatient letter standard (2017) and the HL7 FHIR Task resource were used as structural anchors only.

## Authoring-time language model

A cloud language model may have helped draft static template text (English first, with Arabic and Urdu drafts) at authoring time. It never received any personal data, any real or realistic couple, result, pedigree or laboratory value, or any real person's name. There is no language model at runtime: reviewed templates, a glossary and a deterministic checker produce and police the text (ADR 0003). AI coding assistants also helped write code and documents in this repository; they received only this repository's files and public sources. ## Not used, and why

- **AVDB (Arab Variation and Disease Burden database).** Its public page (https://avdb-arabgenome.ae/) carries a footer reading "All rights reserved" and states no terms (checked 2026-11-20).
- **Jain et al. 2025 supplement (Genet Med Open).** CC BY-NC-ND, which bars commercial reuse and adapted tables.
- **GeneReviews and OMIM.** GeneReviews allows non-commercial research use without modification, and OMIM needs a commercial licence, both per search summaries (Rishta Safe authoring note). Neither is ingested.
- **gnomAD.** Not used. Its licence was not verified on its own pages, and its Middle Eastern group is small and not Emirati (Rishta Safe authoring note, pending clinical sign-off).
- **Published patient-reported instruments** (Decisional Conflict Scale, Decision Regret Scale, GCOS-24, MMIC, Newest Vital Sign). They are free only on conditions such as no profit and no alteration, or no licence terms were found, or they are "all rights reserved" (Rishta Safe authoring note). None is shipped.
- **The standards' forms, risk statements, report layouts and gene list.** Not copied (see above).
- **Emirati Genome Programme data, Malaffi, the EHS app, TAMM and the national genomic database.** No access and no integration. Every adapter is simulated and labelled "simulated" (Rishta Safe authoring note).
- **Other public entries and speech models.** Other public entries to the challenge were read for a positioning note only; no code, text or threshold was copied. No speech model or audio is used or collected.

## Fonts and open-source libraries

Every library used is listed with its version and licence, and every font with its licence, in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` at the repository root. No library or font with a non-commercial or research-only licence is intended to ship.

The runtime dependencies, their versions and licences are listed in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` (26 Python packages and 99 JavaScript packages in the production sets). No dependency ships third-party data or model weights.
